ncviewer

Where is your simulation's output?

Say where the netCDF files of a simulation are. ncviewer finds every file, works out its file type (the files that differ only in their dates), and reads the variables and times they hold. Nothing is changed.

File filters (optional)

Missing values (optional)

Numbers in the files that mean there is no data, such as 0 or -999, and are not marked as missing in them. Give one number, or fill in To as well for a range (both ends are included). They apply to every variable.
